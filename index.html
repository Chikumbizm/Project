Academic Programs

Pathways to Success

Explore our diverse range of educational programs designed for every stage of learning

Language Arts

Building strong reading, writing, and communication skills through engaging literature and interactive activities.

  • Phonics & Reading Comprehension
  • Creative Writing Workshops
  • Public Speaking

Mathematics

Developing logical thinking and problem-solving abilities through hands-on math experiences.

  • Conceptual Understanding
  • Mental Math Strategies
  • Real-world Applications

Science Discovery

Exploring the wonders of science through experiments, observation, and discovery-based learning.

  • Hands-on Experiments
  • Nature Exploration
  • STEM Projects

Advanced Sciences

Biology, Chemistry, and Physics courses with laboratory components for deeper understanding.

  • Full Laboratory Facilities
  • Science Olympiad Prep
  • Research Opportunities

Technology & Coding

Introduction to computer science, programming fundamentals, and digital literacy.

  • Python & JavaScript Basics
  • Web Development
  • Robotics Club

Social Studies

Understanding history, geography, civics, and cultures from around the world.

  • World History
  • Geography & Culture
  • Model UN Program

College Prep

Comprehensive guidance for college applications, standardized tests, and academic planning.

  • SAT/ACT Preparation
  • College Counseling
  • Essay Writing Support

AP & Honors

Advanced Placement courses across multiple disciplines for college-level challenge.

  • 20+ AP Courses
  • Honors Track
  • Dual Enrollment

Career & Technical

Practical skills training for career readiness and vocational exploration.

  • Business & Entrepreneurship
  • Health Sciences
  • Engineering Pathways

Fine Arts

Visual arts, music, theater, and dance programs to nurture creative expression.

  • Studio Art & Design
  • Band & Choir
  • Drama Productions

Athletics

Competitive sports programs and fitness education for physical development.

  • 15+ Varsity Sports
  • Fitness & Wellness
  • Intramural Leagues

World Languages

Developing fluency and cultural understanding through immersive language study.

  • Spanish, French, Mandarin
  • Cultural Exchange Programs
  • Language Immersion
