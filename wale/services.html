Construction

Civil, water and building works

Testing and quality checks are built into every stage of the work.

Sewer systems

We set up a 12km sewer network from scratch, through to lateral mains and house connections.

  • Survey and alignment: benchmark datum lines, total stations and laser levels set the slope and invert levels.
  • Excavation and shoring: mechanical deep trenches with timber and steel shoring to protect the crew.
  • Pipe laying and jointing: PVC sewer pipes laser-aligned and sealed with gaskets or butt-fusion welds.
  • Manholes: cast in place or precast, benched and fitted with heavy-duty cast iron covers.
  • Testing: air or hydrostatic pressure tests and smoke or exfiltration tests before handover.

Water distribution pipelines

Pressurised water mains laid on proper bedding and backfilled in controlled layers.

  • Bedding: a uniform 150mm layer of granular sand or gravel to spread the load.
  • Pipe laying: pressure-rated pipes, mechanical couplings and concrete thrust blocks at bends and tees.
  • Layered backfilling: 150mm loose layers, with oversized stones removed to protect the pipe.
  • Compaction: rammers and vibratory plate compactors to at least 95% Modified Proctor Density.
  • Verification: sand-cone density tests confirm the soil can carry its load.

Structural concrete slabs

Slabs prepared from the ground up, protected against moisture and termites.

  • Subgrade: vegetation cleared, levels cut or filled, then mechanically compacted.
  • Protection: anti-termite treatment and a damp-proof membrane with 150mm sealed overlaps.
  • Formwork: rigid timber or steel formwork checked for plumb, squareness and position.
  • Reinforcement: rebar or BRC mesh on spacer blocks to give the designed concrete cover.
  • Pouring and curing: poker vibration, screeded and power-floated finish, 7-day curing.

Wastewater drying beds

Sloped containment basins with graded filter media for treating sewage sludge.

  • Excavation: sloped-bottom basins dug to the civil specification.
  • Containment walls: reinforced blockwork or cast-in-place concrete with crystalline waterproofing.
  • Underdrains: vitrified clay or perforated PVC lines carry filtered effluent to treatment.
  • Filter media: 15–25mm coarse gravel, fine gravel, then silica sand (0.3–0.7mm).

Building construction

Vertical construction from foundations to roof.

  • Foundations: strip or pad footings with blinding concrete, walls up to damp-proof course level.
  • Blockwork: load-bearing brick or hollow block in 1:3 or 1:4 cement-sand mortar, with wire mesh every third course.
  • Structural frame: cast lintels, columns and ring-beams that tie the walls together.
  • Roofing: treated timber or light-gauge steel trusses fixed with galvanised hoop iron ties.

Painting and finishes

Durable coatings prepared and applied for Zambian weather.

  • Preparation: pressure washing or wire brushing, filling cracks, alkali-resistant primer.
  • Wall painting: multiple coats of UV-resistant acrylic emulsion for even colour and weather resistance.
  • Roof coatings: de-rusting, zinc-phosphate primer and elastomeric roof coatings.
