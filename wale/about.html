About us

A Zambian company building water and civil infrastructure

We are based in Chingola and Luanshya and work with councils, water utilities and mines across the Copperbelt.

Who we are

Quality work, genuine parts, dependable people

Wale Heights Investments Limited is a Zambian registered company specialising in construction, civil engineering, water infrastructure solutions and general supply. We deliver every project to a high standard and to the requirements of our clients and partners.

We supply genuine products and equipment and quality services, so our clients can grow and become more profitable.
