How we work

From survey to handover

The same disciplined sequence on every pipeline and manhole job we take on.

1

Survey

Benchmark datum lines, total stations and laser levels set the slope and invert levels.

2

Excavate

Deep trenches dug mechanically and shored with timber and steel to protect the crew.

3

Lay and joint

Pipes laid on proper bedding, laser-aligned and sealed with gaskets or butt-fusion welds.

4

Build manholes

Cast in place or precast, benched and fitted with heavy-duty cast iron covers.

5

Test

Air, hydrostatic and exfiltration tests, with sand-cone density checks on the backfill.
